Daily Habits

A simple, private habit tracker. Check off your habits each day and watch your streaks grow.

Features

✓ One tap a day

Tap a day to check off a habit. See the whole week at a glance.

🔥 Streaks

Every habit shows how many days in a row you've kept it up.

🔒 Private

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your data never leaves your phone.

Support

Add a habit: type its name at the bottom of the screen and tap Add.

Check off a day: tap the circle under that day. Tap it again to undo.

Delete a habit: press and hold it, then tap Delete.

Where is my data? Only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
