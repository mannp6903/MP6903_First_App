Daily Habits – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Daily Habits is a simple habit tracker made by Mann Patel. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Data stored on your device

The habits you create and the days you check off are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device. Deleting a habit, or uninstalling the app, permanently removes it.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it is safe for users of all ages.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
